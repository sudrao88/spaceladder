Privacy Policy

Space & Ladders · Last updated: 16 May 2026

Space & Ladders (“the App”) is a neon cyberpunk reimagining of Snakes & Ladders, available on the web, iOS, and Android. We built it as a small, fun game and we designed it to collect as little information as possible. This page explains, in plain language, what that means.

Short version

  • We do not require an account. There is no sign-up and no login.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or behavioural tracking.
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs in the App.
  • Game state lives on your device. Nothing about how you play is sent to us.

Information we do not collect

The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, or any other personal identifiers. There is no in-app account system, no leaderboard server, and no social features that upload data about you.

Information that is processed locally on your device

To make the game work, the App stores small pieces of game state on your device, for example: the current board position of each player, whether Math Mode is enabled, and the result of your last roll. This information never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Google Cast (Chromecast)

If you choose to cast the game to a Chromecast-enabled TV or display, the App uses the Google Cast SDK to discover devices on your local network and to send game state (such as the current board, dice value, and player positions) to the receiver running on that device. This communication happens between your phone and your Cast device. The Google Cast SDK is provided by Google and is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive a copy of this data.

Web version & hosting

The web version of the App is hosted on Firebase Hosting. Like virtually every website, the hosting infrastructure automatically receives standard request information (such as your IP address, browser user-agent string, and the URL you requested) in order to serve the page. These logs are managed by Google and are used for security, abuse prevention, and basic operation of the hosting service. We do not maintain a separate analytics layer on top of them. See Firebase’s privacy and security information for details.

Platform services (iOS & Android)

When you install the App from the App Store or Google Play, the platform itself may collect installation, diagnostic, and crash information per Apple’s and Google’s standard policies. We do not run our own crash-reporting or telemetry SDK inside the App. If your device sends crash reports to the platform, those are governed by the platform’s privacy policy, not this one.

Permissions the App requests

  • Network access — used to discover Cast devices on your local network and to load the web build. The App does not need internet to play offline.
  • Display over other apps / wake lock — on some platforms, used to keep the screen awake during a game.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, photos, precise location, or files.

Cookies

The App does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies for advertising or analytics. The web build may use localStorage to remember in-game settings (such as whether Math Mode is on) so they persist between sessions. This data stays in your browser.

Children

Space & Ladders is intentionally family-friendly and suitable for children. Because we do not collect any personal information and do not run advertising or behavioural tracking, the App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has somehow provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can investigate.

Your rights

Because we do not maintain personal data about you on our servers, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. To remove the small amount of local game state the App stores on your device, uninstall the App or use your platform’s “clear app data” option. If you believe we hold information about you and would like to exercise any data-protection right under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA, or another applicable law, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the App handles data, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date at the top. Material changes will also be reflected in the App’s release notes on the App Store and Google Play.
